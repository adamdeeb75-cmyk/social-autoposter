Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Social AutoPoster is a video scheduling application that helps authorized users manage and publish their own short-form videos.

Information We Process

When connected to TikTok, the application may process account identifiers, authorization tokens, video files, captions, and publishing schedules.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to authenticate connected accounts, organize scheduled videos, and submit publishing requests authorized by users.

Data Storage

The current application stores uploaded videos and scheduling information locally on the user's computer. TikTok integration is under development. Storage and security practices will be updated before account authorization is enabled.

Third Parties

Once enabled, TikTok publishing will involve transferring authorized video content and related information to TikTok under its applicable terms.

Data Deletion

Users may request assistance with removing their stored application data or disconnecting an account through the contact method provided by the application operator.
